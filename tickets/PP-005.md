## Objective and scope

Prepare and seek an explicit decision on the initial taxonomy seed, then resolve which immutable completed classification attempt is authoritative for a feedback record when multiple completed attempts exist. Align AI_SPEC.md, DATA_MODEL.md, and DETECTION_SPEC.md so classification counts and detection inputs use one approved rule.

The evidence must distinguish the PRD's proposed taxonomy examples and AI_SPEC candidate code spellings from approved seed content. For attempt authority, preserve immutable attempts and non-additive counting; the highest-attempt-number proposal remains unapproved. No rule is chosen in this ticket before Product Owner disposition.

This ticket does not select a model identifier (PP-033), approve DAI-06 coverage parameters or PB-01 detection thresholds (PP-060), activate a taxonomy/rule, or enable detection, incidents, or notifications.

## Decisions to be resolved

- Initial taxonomy seed: the approved DAI-04 governance rules apply, but the initial category/code content is not approved. Provide a bounded candidate seed and evidence for explicit Product Owner disposition.
- Classification-attempt authority: decide the authoritative completed result when a feedback record has multiple completed attempts, including how a later non-definitive attempt affects the selected result. The highest-attempt-number approach in DETECTION_SPEC is a candidate only; attempts are never additive.
- DAI-05's evaluation-led model selection process is approved; the model identifier remains for PP-033 after development data exists.
- DAI-06 methodology is approved, but exact coverage scope/completeness/calibration and PB-01 thresholds remain Pending Calibration for PP-060. They are not decisions in this ticket.

## Testable acceptance criteria

1. A candidate initial taxonomy seed is documented against PRD requirements and DAI-04 governance, with versioned categories/issue-code and logical-defect identity implications explained; examples and candidate spellings are not presented as approved.
2. Attempt-authority evidence defines the decision question and options, trade-offs, and effects on counts/coverage/fingerprints for zero, one, or multiple completed attempts and relevant retry outcomes. It does not add attempts together or preselect the highest attempt number.
3. The Product Owner explicitly approves, rejects, defers, or leaves each decision unresolved. A recommendation or sample seed is not approval.
4. If a disposition changes or approves behavior, AI_SPEC.md, DATA_MODEL.md, DETECTION_SPEC.md, and the Decision Register record the same selection and status. If unresolved/deferred, all affected downstream gates remain explicit.
5. PRD AC05/AC24 behavior is preserved: validated definitive completions are the only confirmed-count inputs; failed, uncertain, pending, or needs-review outcomes do not become NO_DEFECT. Detection activation remains blocked by applicable PP-060 and PP-009 approvals.
6. The model identifier, detection thresholds, exact DAI-06 calibration parameters, taxonomy activation, and application code/migrations remain outside this ticket.
