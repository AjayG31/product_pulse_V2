## Objective and scope

Resolve the remaining authentication, session, account lifecycle, email canonicalization, rate-limit, CSRF/CORS, bootstrap, and security-audit details. Align SECURITY.md and the API contract while preserving the already approved SEC-01 authentication approach and SEC-02 authorization boundary.

In scope are exact password/session/cookie and CSRF values; the first-Admin setup boundary; account onboarding and recovery policy; email normalization/uniqueness; rate-limit policy; CORS assumptions tied to the deployment model; and security-event audit scope and safe fields. No value or recovery flow is selected by this ticket unless the Product Owner explicitly disposes of it.

## Decisions to be resolved

- SEC-01 is approved for secure email/password authentication, server-side sessions, secure password hashes, Secure and HttpOnly cookies, CSRF protection, expiry/logout invalidation, protected first-Admin setup, Admin-managed users, no public self-registration, and no SSO. Exact settings and account recovery remain open.
- SEC-02 is approved for Admin and Business User roles, single-retailer access, backend authorization, and permission tests. Resolve only the remaining endpoint/audit implementation scope without expanding roles or tenant scope.
- Resolve password scheme/policy and initial-password handling; session expiry, renewal/rotation, cookie attributes/name, concurrent sessions and revocation; first-Admin setup input/guard; account recovery boundary; email canonicalization; numeric rate limits; pre-auth CSRF acquisition and CORS origin assumptions; and security-event audit scope.
- Do not authorize real email or invent password/session/rate-limit values.

## Testable acceptance criteria

1. Every in-scope open item is listed with its owning contract, options, evidence, risks/trade-offs, and recommendation or explicit unresolved status.
2. The proposed choices preserve the approved authentication and RBAC boundaries, including backend enforcement and no Business User ingestion rights.
3. Account recovery and initial-password delivery are explicitly bounded; no email or delivery channel is assumed or authorized without the applicable approval.
4. Email canonicalization/uniqueness, session/cookie/CSRF/CORS behavior, rate limits, and audit events are specified consistently across SECURITY.md, API_SPEC.yaml, and DATA_MODEL.md where each applies. Sensitive values remain excluded from audit snapshots and errors.
5. SECURITY.md, the Decision Register, API_SPEC.yaml, and affected readiness notes agree on each Product Owner disposition. Unresolved values remain visibly open and PP-012/PP-029 stay gated for them.
6. No authentication code, configuration default, or production credential is created as part of the decision work.
